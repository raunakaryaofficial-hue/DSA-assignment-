Algorithm: Merge Two Sorted Linked Lists

1. Initialize head pointers of both sorted lists, L1 and L2.
2. Compare the current nodes: pick the smaller node and add it to the merged list.
3. Advance the pointer of the chosen list.
4. Repeat steps 2 and 3 until one list becomes NULL.
5. Append the remaining elements of the non-empty list to the merged list and return the head.
